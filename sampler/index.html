Sampler

Your photo, as a chart you can stitch.

Pick a photo of a pet, a person, a place or a drawing. Sampler matches every colour to a real stranded-cotton floss, reduces the picture to the number of threads you choose, and draws a counted cross-stitch chart with a symbol for every thread.

Decide before you cut the fabric

Choose your fabric count and Sampler shows the finished size, the piece of fabric to cut, and how many skeins each shade needs. The floss list shares as plain text, ready for the shop.

Made on your device

The photo is charted on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and it works in Airplane Mode.

Colour numbers refer to DMC® stranded cotton for reference; Sampler is not affiliated with DMC.
