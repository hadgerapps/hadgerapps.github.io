What it is

Soft Day is an iPhone app for people who tried gratitude journals and quit because daily logging felt like another chore. Write one good moment on a good day. On a hard day, the app gives it back — gently.

Two-step ritual

Good days. The app asks once: "Anything good today?" Tap Yes, write one sentence, save. That's it.

Hard days. Tap "I need support." A past good moment appears, with a soft line beside it.

Kind by design

  • No streaks. No badges. No reminders that punish you for missing.
  • No AI prompts. No coach voice. The app speaks like a close friend.
  • Voice input — transcribed entirely on your device.
  • Your words stay on your iPhone. We do not have a server.

Subscription

Five free entries to try the full experience. After that, $0.99/week with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Existing entries always stay readable, even if subscription lapses.
