Common questions

Where is my progress stored? Only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded.

Does Postern need an internet connection? No. Only purchases and Restore Purchases need the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription? In your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Postern Pro. Every level stays playable either way.

Why can't I use the Forge's language model? It needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, the Forge still builds a vault from the words you type.
