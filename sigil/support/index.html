SIGIL Support

Questions or bugs? Email hadger.support@gmail.com and a real person will reply.

Someone sent me a glowing circle

It is a sentence. Install SIGIL, open the app, and import that image from Photos, Files or the share sheet; the words come back on your own phone. If the sender wrote it inside one of their circles you will need that circle's key, which they give you once. There is no other way to read it: no web reader, no paste-in page, no API. That is deliberate.

It says "this sigil is for a circle you're not in"

That is the app working, not failing. The mark was written in a circle whose key you do not hold. Ask the person who sent it to add you; you exchange the key once, and every mark in that circle opens after that.

It says "no sigil found here"

The image does not contain a readable mark. The usual causes, in order of how often they happen: a screenshot of a screenshot, an image a chat app has re-compressed several times, or a crop that cut into the outer ring. Ask for the original file, or ask for it to be sent as a document rather than as a photo.

Photographing a mark off someone else's screen does work, but hold the phone square-on to it. Past roughly twenty degrees of tilt, or with a lamp reflecting off the glass, SIGIL may tell you it could not read the image rather than guess; take the offer to try harder, which runs a slower pass that corrects for the angle. It never shows you a phrase it is not sure of.

How do I make a circle?

Name it, then hand the key to the people you want in it: as a key-glyph (the key itself drawn as a SIGIL), by AirDrop or QR, or by reading out the twelve words. Everyone holding that key can both read and write in the circle. It is a shared room, not a one-way channel, so give the key out the way you would give out a spare key to a flat.

My phrase was cut off

A mark holds 145 characters, and that is the same at every tier. The paid tier does not buy a longer phrase.

Can I read a sigil without installing the app?

No, and that is deliberate. There is no web decoder, no reader page and no API. The only way to read a mark is in SIGIL.

I hit "more tomorrow at midnight"

Free includes unlimited reading of open marks, and the first circle you make with unlimited writing and reading. Everything past that counts against 13 a day, which resets at your local midnight. Nothing you received is lost while you wait: a mark you could not read stays in History and opens at the reset, with no re-import. SIGIL Premium lifts the limit.

What is the link in the caption when I share?

Attribution, nothing more. It points at this site so whoever sees your mark can find the app; it does not carry your phrase and it cannot decode anything. Delete it before you send if you would rather it were not there.

I am getting a new phone

Export your keys first: it is free, at every tier, always. Without a key there is no recovery, because we hold nothing, and neither does anyone else.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. Turn on Airplane Mode and use the whole app. The only network call in SIGIL is App Store purchases.

Does it run on iPad?

Yes, in portrait, as the same instrument at the same size rather than a stretched phone screen.

Restore purchases

Settings → Restore Purchases. No account and no email are involved; it is handled by Apple. The Lifetime tier is family-shareable.

What do you know about me?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no server, no identifier. Read the Privacy Policy; it is short.
