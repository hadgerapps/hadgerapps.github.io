Aether Support

Questions or bugs? Email hadger.support@gmail.com and a real person will reply.

The widget shows an older field? Widgets refresh on the system's schedule, not on demand; opening the app updates them immediately. Everything is generated locally, so no connection is needed.

Can Aether set my phone background for me? No app can — iOS has no API for it. Aether renders the field at your device resolution, saves it to Photos, and walks you through setting it in three steps.

Do I need an Apple Intelligence iPhone? No. Every iPhone on iOS 26 draws the field with the built-in renderer. Apple Intelligence, where present, only reads optional text into rendering parameters.

Restore purchases: Settings → Restore Purchases. Lifetime is family-shareable.
