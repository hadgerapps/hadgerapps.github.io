Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026.

Sampler collects no data

Sampler does not collect, transmit or share any information about you. Our App Privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Your photo stays on your device

When you choose a photo, Sampler decodes it on your iPhone or iPad into a working copy no larger than 2048 pixels on its longest side. That copy and the chart made from it are stored only inside the app's own storage on your device, with the project they belong to. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted, and charting works in Airplane Mode.

No permissions, no account, no analytics

Sampler uses Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you select; the app asks for no permissions. There is no sign-up and no account. Sampler contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project deletes its photo copy and its chart. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
