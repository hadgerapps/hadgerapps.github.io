Postern

Find the gap in the wall.

Each vault holds about two thousand tiny bricks behind gray walls that never break. Look for the way in: the gap in a ring, the mouth of a capped pipe, the gate that opens when you break the gold key.

Every doorway doubles the ball

Each time a ball crosses a doorway on its way in, it splits in two. The deeper you get, the more balls tear through the vault.

Built on your phone

Every level is generated on your device from a seed, so level 37 is the same vault for everyone, and so is today's Daily Vault. The bonuses are generated too.

Questions

Are there ads? No. There are no ads, no account and no tracking.

Does it need a connection? No. The game runs on your iPhone or iPad. Only purchases talk to the App Store.

What does Postern Pro add? More lives every five levels, an opening perk on every level, more hidden capsules, unlimited scored Daily Vault runs, and the Forge, which builds a vault from a sentence with Apple's on-device model.

Which devices does it run on? iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later.
