Postern Pro

Postern Pro adds more lives, an opening perk on every level, more hidden capsules, unlimited scored Daily Vault runs and the Forge. It is available as an annual or weekly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase that never renews, at the prices shown in the app before you buy. The annual plan may include a free trial for new subscribers, as stated in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the same price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Every level and every wall plan stay playable without any purchase.

The Forge

On a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on, the Forge reads your sentence with Apple's on-device model. On any other device, or while the model is not yet available, the Forge builds the vault from the words of your sentence.
