Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026.

Postern collects no data

Postern does not collect, transmit or share any information about you. Our App Privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Your game stays on your device

Levels, bonuses and your progress are made and stored on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded. The game needs no connection.

The Forge

When you describe a vault in the Forge, your sentence is read by Apple's on-device language model on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server.

No permissions, no account, no analytics

Postern asks for no permissions: it does not use the microphone, camera, photos or location. There is no sign-up and no account. Postern contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
