What Sampler is

Sampler turns photos you choose into cross-stitch charts for your own personal use. You are responsible for having the right to use the photos you chart. Sampler has no pattern marketplace and does not publish or share your charts.

Sampler Plus

Sampler Plus adds print-ready PDF export and unlimited saved projects. It is available as a one-time purchase, which never renews, or as an annual or weekly auto-renewing subscription, at the prices shown in the app before you buy. The annual plan may include a free trial for new subscribers, as stated in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the same price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Charts you have already made stay viewable without any purchase.

Floss colours

Colour numbers refer to DMC® stranded cotton for reference; Sampler is not affiliated with DMC. Colour matching is approximate: screens and dye lots vary, so check threads against your chart before you begin.
