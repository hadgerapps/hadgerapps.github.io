SIGIL

A sentence, written as a circle.

Write a line of English. SIGIL draws it as a luminous circular mark you send through any messenger as an ordinary image, and the person on the other end reads your exact words back inside the app.

Made on your iPhone. No account, no server, no network in the loop.

It looks like a language, not like junk

Most ciphers produce noise: a wall of base64 nobody wants in their chat. SIGIL produces a mark. Rings of glyph strokes around a fixed centre, drawn in cold light on black. Something a person will actually send, and something the person receiving it will ask about. That question is the whole point.

Every letter rewrites the whole thing

As you type, the entire circle re-forms on every keystroke. Nobody added that for flourish: changing one character changes every byte underneath, so every ring genuinely does change. You are watching the ciphertext move under your hands.

Open marks, and circles

An open mark is not private. Anyone with SIGIL can read it. That is how a mark travels, and it is the app's only advertisement. A circle is a key you and one person, or you and a group, exchange once, in person or over a channel you already trust. Marks written in a circle read only for the people holding that key. Whoever holds the key can both read and write; a circle is a shared room, not a one-way channel, so hand a key out the way you would hand out a spare key to a flat.

There is no decoder anywhere but the app

No web reader, no paste-in page, no API, no shortcut that hands back plaintext. If someone wants to read a mark, they install SIGIL. This is a deliberate constraint, not a missing feature.

Made on your iPhone

A renderer we wrote draws the mark, on your device. Encryption is Apple's own CryptoKit, and keys live in the iOS Keychain and nowhere else. There is no cloud model, no Private Cloud Compute and no server of ours anywhere in the loop. It works in Airplane Mode. Please check.

Private by construction

  • No account, no login, no server, no sign-in. Nothing you write ever leaves the device.
  • App Privacy label: Data Not Collected. The only network call is App Store purchases.
  • Circle keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. They are never transmitted by us, ever.
  • The only way anything leaves your phone is the system share sheet, by your hand.

What SIGIL is not

SIGIL is not an occult or manifestation tool. It is not a messenger or a social network either: it has no inbox, no contacts and no transport of its own, and sending a mark means handing an image to an app you already have. It is not a secure-messaging product. What you write in a circle is only as protected as the people you gave the key to, so do not use SIGIL for anything whose exposure would harm you.

What free includes

Free is complete in kind: full render quality, real circles, real sharing, and reading open marks with no limit. The first circle you make is unlimited too, to write in and to read. Past that, making a mark, or reading one in a second circle, counts against 13 a day, and you keep two inks and your last 20 marks. Key backup and export are free at every tier, always: losing a phone must not cost you every circle you are in. SIGIL Premium, which lifts the daily limit and adds inks, larger export and unlimited history, is available inside the app; the full terms are on the Terms page.

Questions

Does SIGIL need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no Sign in with Apple. Restoring a purchase needs no account either.
How does the other person read it?
They install SIGIL and import the image from Photos, Files, or the share sheet. If it is an open mark, it reads. If it is a circle mark and they hold the key, it reads. If they do not hold the key, the app says so plainly and shows nothing.
Does it work offline?
Yes, entirely. Only the App Store needs a connection. Turn on Airplane Mode and use the whole app.
Is this a messenger?
No. SIGIL has no inbox, no contacts, no directory and no transport of its own. It makes an image; you send that image with whatever you already use.
How long can a phrase be?
145 characters, the same for everyone. The paid tier does not buy you a longer phrase.
What if I lose my phone?
Export your keys before that happens; it is free and always available. Without a key there is no recovery, by design. We hold nothing.
